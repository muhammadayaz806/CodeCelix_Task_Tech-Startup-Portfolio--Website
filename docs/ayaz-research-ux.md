# Ayaz Deliverables — Research + UX + Homepage

## Target Audience (define)

- **Startups & SMEs** that want to scale operations without scaling headcount.
- **Tech founders / product owners** needing fast automation pilots with clear ROI.
- **Ops / COO / RevOps leads** who care about reliability, approvals, and auditability.

### Primary needs

- Reduce repetitive work (routing, follow-ups, reconciliation, reporting)
- Make AI adoption feel **safe and trustworthy**
- See measurable outcomes quickly (time saved, conversion lift, error reduction)

## User Persona (1)

**Name**: Ayesha Khan  
**Role**: COO at a 40-person B2B SaaS (Karachi / remote team)  
**Context**: Processes are growing faster than the team; ops is firefighting.

### Goals

- Cut manual admin work by 25–40%
- Improve lead response time + customer onboarding consistency
- Deploy automation with approvals (no “black box” AI)

### Pain points

- Too many tools, no single source of truth
- Errors + delays from manual handoffs
- Concern about privacy + hallucinations from generic AI tools

### Success metric

- “If I can show time-saved + fewer escalations in 2 weeks, leadership will fund the rollout.”

## Problem Statement

Ops leaders in growing startups need **reliable AI automation** that is easy to adopt and measure, because current ad-hoc automations are fragile, untrusted, and hard to prove ROI.

## Homepage User Flow (primary conversion)

**Goal**: Service inquiry → demo request

1. User lands on homepage → sees “AI you can trust” promise (Hero)
2. Scans proof → outcomes (Case Studies) + credibility (Testimonials)
3. Checks fit → Services + how it works (Services / Process)
4. Converts → fills “Service inquiry” form (Contact section)
5. Optional: newsletter signup CTA for users not ready to contact

## Homepage Wireframe (section map)

Low-fidelity structure (Desktop + Mobile):

1. **Navbar** (sticky): anchor links + CTA buttons
2. **Hero**: headline, subtext, 2 CTAs, “dashboard” visual
3. **Trusted-by strip**: logos/badges
4. **Services**: 3 cards (AI automation, copilots, integrations)
5. **Features / value props**: 4 tiles
6. **How it works**: 3-step process
7. **Case Studies**: 3 outcome cards (result-first)
8. **Testimonials**: 2 quotes
9. **Blog preview**: 3 article cards
10. **Newsletter CTA**: email capture
11. **Contact**: inquiry form + “what happens next”
12. **Footer**: links + contact
